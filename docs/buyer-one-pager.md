# RightOrder — buyer one-pager

## What is being sold

RightOrder is a pre-revenue crypto-arbitrage SaaS codebase. It is offered as a technical asset and a launch head start, not as a revenue-producing business or an investment product.

## Included product capabilities

- TypeScript/Node.js backend with exchange-spread scanning and strategy modules.
- React/Vite dashboard for market spreads and operational workflows.
- Authentication, TOTP 2FA, subscription and payment integration layers.
- PostgreSQL/Prisma data layer, Docker Compose deployment, CI and automated tests.
- Deployment documentation and a security/architecture audit for a qualified buyer during diligence.

## Verified handover state

- Backend typecheck passes.

- A local, read-only product preview is available during a live technical demo.

## Honest status and remaining work

- The project has not been launched to paying users; no revenue, customers or trading profits are represented.
- Production credentials, payment configuration and exchange connectivity are not included and must be replaced by the buyer.
- The current VPS deployment is not part of the default sale and is unavailable as a public demo. A buyer receives a reproducible local deployment path instead.
- A buyer should complete its own legal, security and market-data review before launch.
